## Current project status

Recommended Training Pathways v0.3 is a separate maintained deliverable. Foundation/L1 uses TR-281, TR-145 and the one-hour internal **AI use cases** brief and is **Reviewed**. L2, L3, specialist and coached paths are **AI recommendation - to be reviewed**. TR-229 Reasoning with o1 is excluded from these recommendations. The OWASP, NIST AI RMF and MITRE ATLAS core resource placements are Reviewed; their wider pathways remain pending. The full scenario remains Draft and is published in learner 1.6.3. Each scrolling learning section now separates **Central recommendations** from optional **Suggested for you** matches, while retaining the four progress cards. Import the pathway into the planner to work with the maintained choices.

The catalogue and both apps use v17 evidence, including the reviewed Udemy SecAI+ courses and the saved AI use cases internal brief in its own worksheet. The builder's component workspace starts blank. By subsequent user request, the staff app now publishes Recommended Training Pathways v0.3. The current legacy planner bundle removes retired TR-229; no replacement is assigned. Catalogue v17 has 516 active resources after 18 approved retirements. V17 simplifies the workbook from 18 tabs to 12; obsolete planning views are preserved in archived v16. All imported course records and assessments are unchanged. Historical course evidence, workbooks and app distributions remain intact. Saved personal plans and feedback remain preserved.

The outdated 2027 plan and the no-longer-needed Foundation/L1 review are [archived](archive/README.md). Future planning belongs in [analysis](analysis/README.md). Four PowerPoint decks are live, including the seven-slide framework introduction added on 4 November 2026. The original briefing's historical catalogue appendix needs a freshness review before it is described as aligned with the current catalogue.
