## How to use the pathways

Compose Foundation + the shared learning through the person’s agentic level + relevant role responsibilities + approved tool training. The same course or internal activity is counted once. Levels follow the work, independently of seniority; demonstrated capabilities need not be retaught. Specialist audience levels below are proposed starting points, not requirements for everyone in a role.

**Reviewed** records the user’s review of Foundation/L1 on 1 November 2026 and the core reference placements below on 2 November. Approval of those resource choices does not approve the rest of a component or pathway. It does not declare full outcome coverage, delivery readiness, competence or operational permission. The full scenario remains Draft. Review labels are descriptive and do not add planner gates.

Course time uses the full catalogue duration or the upper bound of a range. ATLAS and the NIST bundle have unknown durations; totals show the known subtotal plus resources with time to confirm. A shortened excerpt cannot inherit full-resource coverage. Internal learner time is additional. Product training is still unselected and its duration is unknown; it must be added before a complete delivery commitment is made. Time budgets and calendar dates are unset. Suggested facilitators in historical briefs are not assigned owners.

Assessed coverage describes catalogue syllabus evidence. Planned coverage additionally assumes full teaching of outcomes linked to internal activities, including Proposed activities. Neither percentage measures learner competence. Zero must-have gaps means no applicable must-have has a zero score; partially covered must-haves still need attention.
