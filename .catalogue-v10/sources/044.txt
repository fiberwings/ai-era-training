What you'll learn
Master a step-by-step framework for the development of AI projects.

Explore real-world case studies related to public health, climate change, and disaster management.
About this certificate
The AI for Good Professional Certificate is a three-course program from DeepLearning.AI that teaches you how to apply AI to some of society's most pressing challenges. You'll work through real projects spanning public health, climate change, and disaster management, designing and building solutions alongside the practitioners who deploy them in the field.
At the heart of the specialization is a four-phase project framework — Explore, Design, Implement, and Evaluate — that you'll apply repeatedly across diverse problem domains. Each project walks you through the full lifecycle: engaging stakeholders, defining the problem, determining whether AI is actually the right tool, prototyping a model, and evaluating real-world impact.
Beyond the technical skills, the specialization develops the judgment needed to scope AI projects responsibly. You'll examine case studies from leading researchers and practitioners, think critically about data privacy and ethical deployment, and learn how to communicate results to non-technical stakeholders. By the end, you'll have a portfolio of completed projects and a repeatable framework you can apply to AI for Good problems in your own community or organization.
Learn through real-world projects
Develop an AI model to make wind power generation more predictable by providing forecasts 24 hours into the future.
Use neural networks and other AI techniques to estimate air quality throughout the city of Bogotá, Colombia.
Build an image classification pipeline to perform damage assessment using satellite images taken after Hurricane Harvey in the U.S. in 2017.
Use natural language processing techniques to analyze trends in a corpus of text messages sent in the aftermath of the 2010 earthquake in Haiti.
Apply computer vision techniques to detect and classify animals for the purpose of biodiversity monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why should I take this specialization?
Whether you’re a student, professional, or someone passionate about making a positive impact on society and the environment, this program provides the tools and knowledge you will need to work on AI for Good initiatives.
What background knowledge is necessary for AI for Good?
This is a beginner-friendly specialization with minimal background knowledge required. Some prior experience in working with data (spreadsheets, etc) is recommended. Python will be used for ungraded labs, but previous experience is not required. This is a great place to start if you’re new to AI and want to learn how it can be applied to real-world challenges.
I don’t have any programming experience, can I take this specialization?
Yes. Programming experience is not required to take this course.
I have intermediate Python experience, is this specialization for me?
If you’re looking to apply those skills in an AI for Good project, then yes! Although the labs for this specialization will be beginner-level, you’ll be able to dig into the code behind each case study and get more creative and manipulate the data and models used in this specialization in more advanced ways.
I’m looking for a course that will help me build my technical skills in AI, is AI for Good for me?
If you’re looking for only AI skill-building, we recommend starting with AI for Everyone or the Machine Learning Specialization. AI for Good is designed to build conceptual skills in AI, and focuses on their use in AI for Good contexts.
Do I need to take the courses in a specific order?
We recommend taking the courses in the prescribed order for a logical and consistent learning experience.
How much does the DeepLearning.AI Pro membership cost?
The DeepLearning.AI Pro membership costs $25/mo billed annually and $30/mo billed monthly.
