2027 execution

Build and pilot before scaling

Q1 · Establish

Confirm profiles, tool access and prerequisites. Nominate case and content owners. Pilot the Foundation and L1 work case; deliver them to the workforce. Hold the first leadership evidence forum.

Exit evidence: approved roster, verified use cases, safe platform walkthrough and knowledge/work-case results.

Q2 · Supervise

Deliver L2 courses and four bank exercises. Pilot Skills ownership, evaluation and recovery. Run workflow/leadership studios and the second forum. Prepare L3 and role sandboxes.

Exit evidence: observed supervision, tested Skills and reproducible domain benchmarks.

Q3 · Build and specialise

Deliver L3 only to builders and the relevant role branches to their practitioners. Run risk cases, identity drills, adversarial tests and harness benchmarks. Hold the third forum.

Exit evidence: independent challenges, role artifacts, tracked remediation and separate operational approvals.

Q4 · Verify and refresh

Complete reassessments, review adoption and incidents, retire stale Skills and refresh platform exercises. Hold the fourth forum and use results to plan the next year.

Exit evidence: completion and performance records, risk/benefit decisions and the next catalogue review.

The full set of 27 reusable internal module briefs implies {{BUILD}} hours of one-time development if every recommended branch is activated. These are planning estimates, not supplier quotes, and exclude repeated facilitation, learner time, assessment delivery, platform engineering and licences. Prioritise the profiles actually assigned; do not reserve every branch by default.

Calculate workforce learner hours from a named roster: for each person, sum their unique courses and internal module IDs, then add any separately assigned product onboarding and remediation. Confirm current enterprise entitlements, external course access, paid labs/assignments, API usage and seats before procurement. Capability model and method

Independent levels, responsibilities and tool access

{{MODEL}}
Nine role families and their outcome mapping{{ROLEMAP}}

For each applicable requirement, take the maximum assessed score among unique selected courses: 0 = no evidenced coverage, 1 = mention, 2 = substantive but incomplete teaching, 3 = the complete rubric requirement. Missing assessments remain labelled unassessed. Each need is the mean of its requirement scores divided by three; the profile metric is the equal-weight mean of its applicable needs. Scores are not added across courses.

Full means every applicable requirement scores 3. Strong means at least 70% coverage and no critical zero; Partial means at least 30%; otherwise Weak. The current model changes applicability, not the underlying rubric or scores. Shared layers and role overlaps are removed from the incremental scope after inheritance, while cumulative results use each outcome once.

Global assignments exclude TR-125, TR-128 and TR-129; local national guidance belongs in a separately scoped supplement. TR-159 remains excluded pending a bounded lab estimate. Unavailable resources, including TR-279 pending delivery validation, do not enter the optimiser. Existing full-resource duration estimates are retained for comparison. All source scores, evidence and the v10 workbook remain unchanged.
