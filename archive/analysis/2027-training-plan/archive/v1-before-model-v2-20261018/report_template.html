01 · The decision

Recommend Option 4: quality core and in-house capability

The most valuable internal teaching connects the capability model to the bank’s actual work: credible use cases from colleagues, the tools people can access, the controls they must use, and the workflows they are accountable for. External courses supply reusable technical foundations; internal modules supply this organisational context and an observed demonstration of ability.

Option 4 develops Option 2 with a dedicated N1 showcase, a specified foundation, and structured supervisor labs. A workshop on real workflows replaces the generic organisational-redesign course in Expert and leadership paths. The total remains modest because the internal teaching is integrated into existing practice and assessment time.

Decision condition: select Option 4 if content owners, approved environments and assessor capacity can be committed in Q1. Option 2 is a lower-development alternative, although it still requires bank-specific practice. Option 3 suits a strict sourcing policy. Option 1 is the coverage benchmark.

03 · Build where context matters

Teach our use cases, tools and ways of working

The internal portfolio has three foundation modules, four Practitioner modules and a small number of modules for each Expert or leadership pathway. A learner receives only the modules on their pathway. Reuse cases, scaffolds and assessment evidence across the programme rather than commissioning a separate course for every need.

N1 flagship: what our people are doing with AI — 90 minutes

Make colleagues the presenters. Show two worked cases from different functions and one emerging or restricted-pilot capability. Use a brief gallery to represent the remaining functions. Demonstrate the work before and after, show what failed, explain the human checks, and distinguish measured results from aspirations.

Available now

Name the approved tool, who can access it, the permitted data and the route to request access. Show a repeatable example on the current platform and a failure that required human judgement.

Emerging or restricted

Label the capability as a restricted pilot or horizon demonstration. Explain who currently has access, unresolved constraints and the approval route. A demonstration is not permission to use the tool, nor a promise of wider availability.

Learning evidence: N1.1 — explain a use case in the learner’s own domain and one in a neighbouring domain; N1.2 — distinguish current production capability from emerging capability; N1.3 — identify two or three ways their work could change and where human accountability remains.

Six case briefs for functional heads to source

These are candidate briefs, not claims that the bank has already deployed these solutions. Each head nominates a real case and confirms its owner, access status and evidence. Where a real case does not yet exist, label the example as a proposed or external demonstration.

@@CASES@@

Use a common case card: business task; original workflow; tool and model; data boundary; production/pilot/horizon status; access route; observed result and measurement method; failure and correction; human approval; reusable lesson; case owner; last-reviewed date. Use redacted or approved synthetic material.

Module briefs and assessment design

Hours are estimated learner commitments. Each brief names an owner, prerequisites and the evidence to collect. All modules are proposed and unassessed.

@@DESIGN@@

Production and delivery capacity

Allow an initial @@BUILDLOW@@–@@BUILDHIGH@@ person-hours to design, review and pilot the reusable shared-pathway materials. These are planning allowances, not measured estimates or supplier quotes. They include subject-matter and instructional design effort and pilot repairs; they exclude learner attendance, course delivery, translation, travel, platform engineering and ongoing refresh. Shared workflow-studio design is counted once, with separate role adaptations.

@@BUILD@@

Commission only the specialist branches needed by nominated roles. For delivery planning, budget facilitator time per session and assessor time per learner separately: the 2h Practitioner sign-off is not a single 2h staffing allowance for an entire cohort. Reuse the same workflow studio across roles where practical, while retaining each learner’s attendance in their own plan.

Release rule: an internal module needs an approved syllabus, exact requirement mapping, working case or lab, independent answer key, pilot results, review owner and refresh trigger. Reassess its teaching evidence using the same 0–3 rubric before adding it to the assessed catalogue. Learner competence is then checked separately at the gate.

07 · Functional adaptation

Assign the branch to the responsibility

Option 4 below. Each branch includes 4h of internal work: a 1h runbook/control walkthrough, a 2h coached exercise and a 1h observed challenge. Apply only the branches relevant to the individual. Merge overlapping exercises explicitly when someone holds several roles.

@@SPECIALTIES@@

SOC and threat-intelligence staff use the shared pathway and adopted tools; they do not automatically need forensics. IDP separates DLP/classification from AI risk assessment. BISO separates governance from assurance. Application Security selects testing, exposure management, harness engineering or architecture. IAM distinguishes lifecycle operations from Expert integration. CIO uses strategy and adoption exercises.

08 · Delivery and gates

Protect time and demonstrate ability in stages

Q1 · Build & pilot

Source real cases and approve content

Heads nominate learners and case owners. Confirm available tools, coding readiness and access. Build and pilot IH-01–07; prepare shared case templates and assessment rubrics. Begin leadership decisions.

Q2 · Foundation & supervisors

Teach the common operating baseline

Deliver the 7.8h foundation in short sessions. Nominated supervisors then take TR-100 and four 2h internal modules. Use small cohorts and protected assessment appointments.

Q3 · Role pathways

Work on a real bounded use case

After the supervisor gate, technical and governance candidates follow their selected pathway. Co-deliver workflow studios where useful. Commission only the necessary specialist cases.

Q4 · Challenge & refresh

Test independent judgement

Complete the Expert challenge and remediation; record authorised scope and residual gaps. Review operational evidence and refresh content as tools, models and policies change.
