# Foundation and L1 training review and draft executive recommendation

31 October 2026 · Complete courses, with combined Foundation + L1 options · Cybersecurity workforce

**Including N6 makes a combined Foundation + L1 programme a credible choice and changes the preferred Anthropic course.** For that broader objective, recommend piloting **Anthropic’s complete four-hour AI Fluency: Framework and foundations course, Microsoft’s complete introduction to agents, and newly built bank teaching and practice**. The proposed total is approximately **10 hours including N5 and excluding N1**, spread across self-study, a workshop and five days of short workplace practice. Its strongest advantage is direct teaching and exercises on the four essential L1 capabilities: delegation, context, discernment and accountability.

The efficient alternative uses **Anthony Alicea’s complete Udemy course** and the same Microsoft module, with more internal L1 practice: approximately **eight hours** on the same basis. If the decision remains Foundation only, that Udemy route remains the recommendation at **5h22m**, with six hours reserved. Longer courses should earn their place through relevant practice; adding L1 to the programme title does not make every additional topic useful.

All external assignments below are complete published courses or complete standalone Microsoft Learn modules, including their activities and assessments. No route depends on assigning individual lessons. Microsoft calls its independently published seven-unit learning item a “module”; the proposed assignment is the whole item, not selected units from it. Its assignment and completion-recording method must be checked in the organisation’s learning system.

N1 is excluded from external course selection and from the time comparisons below. It will be covered by the internal showcase of current AI use cases. N2, N3 and N4 remain the Foundation priorities. N5 can be delivered separately rather than driving the external core. N6 adds eight L1 outcomes, including daily use within bank rules; course completion alone does not establish that workplace capability.

**No shortlisted external course demonstrates complete coverage of N2–N4.** Buying or assigning a longer generic AI course would not remove the need to explain approved bank platforms, permitted data and actions, and escalation routes. The proposed internal component also supplies substantive teaching on agent safety and responsible use; it is more than a policy acknowledgement.

## Decision proposed

- **Combined Foundation + L1: pilot Option F, Anthropic AI Fluency plus Microsoft**, at **9h57m estimated learner time**. Its additional curriculum serves the essential N6 outcomes directly. This is the preferred direction where quality and practical transfer take priority over the shortest duration.
- **Efficient combined alternative: Option A+, Udemy plus Microsoft**, at **7h52m**. It uses the existing Udemy entitlement and saves **2h05m per learner**, but relies more on internally designed L1 teaching and on validating the complete Udemy course.
- **Foundation only: retain Option A at 5h22m**, or Option B at **6h57m** for deeper understanding of model limitations. These are not complete L1 programmes.
- **DeepLearning.AI: choose AI Prompting for Everyone over Generative AI for Everyone if L1 is the objective.** The combined Option G is **14h12m**; the multimedia and app-building curriculum needs a positive business justification. Do not require both courses.
- Keep the three-course Udemy route as an operational fallback. Google becomes a more credible combined alternative, but neither is preferred over the two leading routes on the current evidence.

Every option requires new internal materials. These are proposed deliverables, not existing finished courses. N1 is a separate internal showcase, also to be prepared; its time is excluded from the comparisons.

These are proposed learning allocations, not measured completion times. The course review supports a pilot decision; it does not establish improved job performance or certify learner competence.

### What is new in the combined programme

The external courses already exist. **All bank sessions, the application exercise, assessment rubric and workplace-practice process below still need to be built or verified.** The N2 agent bridge (40m), N4 responsible-use session (50m) and N5 awareness session (45m) reuse the proposed Foundation deliverables; they are counted once, not repeated for L1.

- **N3 workshop, 45m for F/G/E+.** A new session covering the seven limitations and counter-habits, with synthetic demonstrations of fabricated claims, leading questions, lost context, repeated-output variation, document injection and over-trust. It compensates for the different external core; it is not a selection of lessons from another course.
- **L1 application, 60m for F, 90m for A+/E+, 75m for G.** A new exercise using an approved assistant and a suitable work task. For F, allocate 15m to model/effort and data/platform choices, 15m to context/memory recovery and 30m to completing and checking a task using the 4Ds. A+/E+ add 30m of structured delegation, discernment and accountability practice. G adds 15m focused on ownership/disclosure. These are design estimates to test, not promises of mastery within the time.
- **Integrated assessment, 30m.** This replaces the Foundation-only 20m assessment. It checks safety decisions and reviews the learner’s task, context, verification and accountability evidence against all eight N6 outcomes. The application creates the work sample; the assessment checks it. Assessor preparation and review effort are additional organisational costs.
- **Daily practice, five working days × 10m = 50m.** A new workplace activity and brief log, with manager/peer feedback. Record the task, approved platform, delegated work, what was checked and any correction. This is protected practice time, not another existing online course. Five days begins a habit; it does not prove sustained daily capability. Confirm continued safe use at the follow-up.

The learning lead should own the application and rubric with the platform/governance owners; line managers should support practice and follow-up. If staff lack approved access or suitable tasks, they can complete simulated practice, but N6.8 should remain pending until appropriate workplace use is demonstrated.

**Timing assumptions.** Anthropic’s four hours and Coursera’s eight hours are treated as whole-course estimates, including ordinary course activities and assessment. Anthropic’s project is described as approximately an hour of work distributed through the course; confirm in the pilot that it fits the four-hour estimate. If it proves additional, F becomes **10h57m**. Its explicitly optional post-course extension suggestions are not extra mandatory assignments; every published lesson and the required course work remain included. [Project guidance](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations/project-planning-and-delegation), [post-course activities](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations/additional-activities).

Use approximately **10–11 hours protected learning time for F during the pilot**, split across days, and measure the actual workload before rollout. If the earlier 90-minute N1 showcase allocation is retained, combined totals become **F 11h27m, A+ 9h22m, G 15h42m and E+ 12h57m** before breaks or additional remediation. Subtract 45 minutes from any combined total if N5 is scheduled outside this programme; it remains a separate Foundation obligation.

## How quality and fit were assessed

The assessment gives priority to accuracy, explicit treatment of the required outcomes, credible authorship, understandable explanations, meaningful practice and efficient use of learner time. Familiar tools help transfer learning, but a product tutorial is not automatically a Foundation course. Ratings are supporting signals, not proof of quality. “Beginner” can still mean a beginner developer rather than an ordinary employee.

Evidence combines the existing v11 catalogue with current official syllabuses and accessible lesson text. Microsoft’s agent, prompt-injection and assessment content, and Anthropic’s delegation, description, discernment, diligence, project and model-behaviour lessons were inspected directly. Udemy’s detailed public outlines and lesson descriptions were checked; its complete paid videos were not watched. DAIR lesson links required sign-in. Some DeepLearning.AI pages could not be retrieved directly, so current Coursera syllabuses and the retained provider evidence were used, with platform differences identified below.

Consequently, “best candidate” means the best-supported choice for a controlled pilot. Final Udemy endorsement requires a trainer to review the complete assigned course, especially its factual explanations and safety advice.

## Quality and impact checks before wider assignment

Run a small pilot with approximately 12–20 people across operational cyber, GRC and engineering responsibilities, including staff with limited AI experience. For the combined objective, use Option F as the lead candidate and A+ as the comparison if capacity permits. For Foundation alone, use A and B. Compare like-for-like objectives and assessments; do not compare F’s time or outcomes with A as though they promised the same scope. This is a practical selection exercise, not a statistically conclusive comparison.

The trainer review should check the actual Udemy videos for factual accuracy, clear distinction between a model and an agent, and sound handling/verification advice. Check the employee responsible-use candidates particularly carefully for overgeneralised regulation and claims about data retention. Test full-course access, captions, activity completion and Microsoft’s exercise from the bank’s environment. Confirm that the whole Microsoft module can be assigned and its completion recorded.

Assess application, not only satisfaction: recognise a fabricated citation, reject an instruction hidden in a document, select the approved route for confidential data, explain missing agent permissions/stop controls, and give the appropriate escalation. Use synthetic information. Require every safety-critical item to be correct after remediation; use a proposed 80% threshold for the wider knowledge check. These thresholds are programme-design proposals, not validated measures of competence.

Record actual study time, confusing explanations, access failures, pre/post scenario results and a short retention check after two to four weeks. A completion badge alone should not be treated as permission to delegate actions to an agent. Choose the longer route only where better learning or a failed quality review justifies its additional time.

For the combined routes, also review an actual work sample: the delegation boundary, supplied context, rejected or corrected output, model/effort and platform choices, context recovery, and disclosure of AI’s role. Use the daily practice log and follow-up to assess N6.8. Require evidence for each essential N6 outcome rather than allowing an average quiz score to hide a missing capability. Where permitted, compare time and output quality on a similar task before and after training; record quality failures as well as speed gains. These are proposed impact checks, not an assertion that the courses have demonstrated productivity gains in this workforce.

## First draft for the executive committee

We recommend piloting a combined Foundation and L1 programme. This would teach colleagues both how AI systems fail and how to use approved assistants with sound judgement, appropriate context and clear personal accountability. N1 will remain an internal showcase of current bank use cases.

The preferred external core is Anthropic’s complete AI Fluency: Framework and foundations course, supported by Microsoft’s complete Introduction to generative AI and agents module. These existing resources require approximately four hours and 40 minutes. Anthropic’s exercises directly support the four essential L1 capabilities: delegation, context, discernment and accountability.

We must also develop the bank-specific agent and responsible-use teaching, a limitations workshop, AI-security awareness, an applied work exercise and an integrated assessment. Five short daily practice sessions will support transfer into work. These internal components are proposed deliverables, not finished courses, and require named owners and preparation time.

The combined programme is estimated at ten hours per learner excluding N1, including N5 and workplace practice. We propose reserving ten to eleven hours during the pilot to test the actual course and project workload. Anthropic Academy and Microsoft Learn content are free; approved product access, internal preparation and learner time remain costs.

An alternative using the complete Alicea Udemy course would reduce the combined estimate to approximately eight hours, with more internal teaching of the L1 skills. It should be retained as the efficient alternative, subject to review of the complete course. If the committee chooses Foundation only, the Udemy programme requires approximately five hours and 25 minutes, with six hours reserved. DeepLearning.AI offers a credible practical route, but the combined estimate exceeds fourteen hours and includes additional building and multimedia work that is not required for every employee.

The committee is asked to endorse the combined pilot, assign owners for the internal materials and provide protected learner time. Rollout should follow evidence of safe decisions and usable work, rather than course completion alone. Foundation and L1 outcomes will be tracked separately within the single programme, so introductory overlap is taught once and daily workplace capability is evidenced before L1 is recorded as achieved.
