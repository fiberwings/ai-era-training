The strongest first recommendation is a short combination of Udemy for AI limitations, Microsoft for the introduction to agents, and internal teaching for agent controls and the bank’s responsible-use rules. Anthropic offers the strongest alternative where deeper learning justifies more time. DeepLearning.AI is credible, but its complete courses fit this Foundation brief less closely than their reputation alone might suggest.

N1 is excluded from external course selection and from the time comparisons below. It will be covered by the internal showcase of current AI use cases. N2, N3 and N4 are the priorities. N5 remains a required Foundation need, but can be delivered separately rather than driving the choice of external core.

No shortlisted external course demonstrates complete coverage of N2–N4. Buying or assigning a longer generic AI course would not remove the need to explain approved bank platforms, permitted data and actions, and escalation routes. The proposed internal component also supplies substantive teaching on agent safety and responsible use; it is more than a policy acknowledgement.

Decision proposed

  • Pilot a curated route using Anthony Alicea’s Udemy course and Microsoft’s introductory agent module. Allow about 4 hours 40 minutes per learner including N5, excluding N1. This includes exercises and a local assessment.
  • If assigning and recording selected lessons is impractical, use the full Udemy course. Allow about 5 hours 25 minutes, on the same basis.
  • Retain Anthropic’s AI capabilities and limitations as the alternative for greater depth. The comparable package is about 6 hours 45 minutes. Choose it if the pilot shows a worthwhile improvement in understanding, or if the Udemy lessons do not meet the quality standard.
  • Do not purchase a broad DeepLearning.AI programme for everyone solely to satisfy Foundation. Consider selected lessons or use its courses later for L1 and specialist development.

These are proposed learning allocations, not measured completion times. The course review supports a pilot decision; it does not establish improved job performance or certify learner competence.

What the training must cover

The authoritative capability model defines 20 Foundation outcomes across N2–N4 and six across N5. N4.7 belongs to L2, N5.7 belongs to the threat intelligence specialism, and N5.2 is retired. Those outcomes are excluded here.

How quality and fit were assessed

The assessment gives priority to accuracy, explicit treatment of the required outcomes, credible authorship, understandable explanations, meaningful practice and efficient use of learner time. Familiar tools help transfer learning, but a product tutorial is not automatically a Foundation course. Ratings are supporting signals, not proof of quality. “Beginner” can still mean a beginner developer rather than an ordinary employee.

Evidence combines the existing v11 catalogue with current official syllabuses and accessible lesson text. Microsoft’s agent, prompt-injection and assessment content, and Anthropic’s discernment, diligence and model-behaviour lessons were inspected directly. Udemy’s detailed public outlines and lesson descriptions were checked; its complete paid videos were not watched. DAIR lesson links required sign-in. Some DeepLearning.AI pages could not be retrieved directly, so current Coursera syllabuses and the retained provider evidence were used, with platform differences identified below.

Consequently, “best candidate” means the best-supported choice for a controlled pilot. Final Udemy endorsement requires a trainer to review the selected videos, especially their factual explanations and safety advice.

Case 1 Udemy already available in the organisation

Best core candidate

AI Literacy for the Modern Workplace (Generative AI Upskill), Anthony Alicea — INT-437. The published runtime is 1h45m. The catalogue records teaching across all seven N3 themes, including sycophancy and hidden instructions. It also records practice and a role-play activity. Current public listing: 4.5/5 from 1,849 ratings; this supports acceptability, not effectiveness. Course

Advantages. Its unusually close N3 match and concise structure make it the strongest Udemy starting point. It builds on an explanation of model behaviour rather than only issuing warnings. It is already identified in the bank’s catalogue and current draft pathway, reducing delivery friction.

Limitations. The v11 assessment gives partial coverage of the N3 outcomes, not full satisfaction. It provides no scored N2 coverage under the complete-outcome rubric and little N4 coverage beyond data/privacy. Workplace productivity and prompting content overlaps L1 and the purpose of the N1 showcase. Public lesson descriptions are not enough to validate every explanation: the privacy teaching must not leave staff thinking that paraphrasing confidential information makes an otherwise prohibited upload acceptable.

Suggested use. Select its fundamentals, risks and decision-framework lessons for Foundation; keep the remaining productivity material available for L1. The proposed playlist appears later in this review. Use the full course if the organisation needs straightforward course-level completion records.

Case 2 DeepLearning.AI

Best complete introductory course

Generative AI for Everyone — Andrew Ng, TR-221. The retained DeepLearning.AI duration is 5h01m; current Coursera estimates six hours. Its public outline includes capabilities and limitations, model selection, cost, RAG, business implications and six assignments. The agent lesson is optional and listed at six minutes; the responsible-AI lesson is five minutes. Current syllabus

Advantages. Clear, structured nontechnical teaching and a credible instructor. Particularly useful for people needing a broad understanding of generative AI and its business context.

Limitations. The full programme spends material time on applications, project lifecycle and business change that this brief discounts or places elsewhere. Its short treatment of agents and responsible use cannot satisfy N2.4, N2.6 or the complete N4 need. The outline does not establish comprehensive teaching of the seven N3 failures. High-quality broad education; not the best mandatory Foundation fit. The extra time makes sense if the executive committee deliberately broadens the objective to business literacy.

Case 3 Anthropic and Microsoft

Anthropic

AI capabilities and limitations — TR-145, currently 3½ hours. This is the strongest candidate for deeper understanding of N3. Its current course describes four model properties and includes exercises and a quiz. It explicitly names sycophancy, context limits and failure diagnosis. Course

The accessible How AI Gets Its Character lesson goes beyond a topic heading: learners compare model responses under different framings and reflect on the resulting behaviour. This is a better signal of potential learning impact than a promise to “understand AI risks.” Inspected lesson

Its disadvantage is time and scope: it develops a model-behaviour mental model rather than the complete agent architecture or employee responsible-use curriculum. Add explicit prompt-injection and bank-policy scenarios. Recommended alternative to the Udemy core when depth matters more than brevity. The extra time is defensible because it buys practice and explanation, rather than unrelated building content.

AI Fluency: Framework and foundations — TR-089, currently four hours. The 4D framework addresses how people delegate, communicate, judge results and take responsibility. Its educators and coherent instructional framework make it a strong candidate for broader working-with-AI capability. Course

The inspected discernment lesson asks learners to evaluate outputs in a domain they know; the diligence lesson develops responsibility and disclosure. These are useful N3 counter-habits and generic N4 support. They do not teach the complete bank risk, regulation and permissions requirements. Best positioned in L1 or for champions. Assigning both Anthropic courses to everyone would mean 7½ hours before bank-specific learning. Discernment, diligence

Claude 101 — currently 2½ hours. Its coverage of projects, Skills and connected tools makes it useful for staff who will use those features. It is principally tool onboarding, with feature access dependent on plan and administrator settings. Use selected relevant lessons in the tooling layer, rather than requiring the entire product tour for Foundation. Course

N5 as a separate awareness session

A proposed 45-minute session can be organised as follows:

Segment Time Outcomes
AI-enabled deception and intrusion 10m Phishing and impersonation, independent-channel verification, AI-assisted intrusion, changing speed/volume: N5.1, N5.3, N5.5
Risks affecting AI systems and agents 20m N5.8: instruction manipulation, disclosure, unsafe output handling, excessive permissions/actions, compromised components/sources, disruption or uncontrolled consumption; one example and precaution for each
Bank and domain scenarios 10m N5.4 and N5.6: a plausible attack on an approved AI workflow and the correct local response
Check and escalation recap 5m Distinguish attack classes and identify the reporting route

This deliberately consolidates the previous internal risk sessions and needs piloting. It is not equivalent to completing the old 60-minute limitations/threats session plus the separate 45-minute risk overview. The public evidence does not establish that Microsoft’s 53-minute security module or Cisco’s tutorial covers all six N5 outcomes. Either can support the trainer or serve as additional learning, but bank attack examples and independent-verification habits remain necessary.

Quality and impact checks before wider assignment

Run a small pilot with approximately 12–20 people across operational cyber, GRC and engineering responsibilities, including staff with limited AI experience. Use the concise route as the lead candidate and test the Anthropic alternative with a comparable small group if capacity permits. This is a practical selection exercise, not a statistically conclusive comparison.

The trainer review should check the actual Udemy videos for factual accuracy, clear distinction between a model and an agent, and sound handling/verification advice. Check the employee responsible-use candidates particularly carefully for overgeneralised regulation and claims about data retention. Test lesson access, captions, selected-lesson tracking and Microsoft’s exercise from the bank’s environment.

Assess application, not only satisfaction: recognise a fabricated citation, reject an instruction hidden in a document, select the approved route for confidential data, explain missing agent permissions/stop controls, and give the appropriate escalation. Use synthetic information. Require every safety-critical item to be correct after remediation; use a proposed 80% threshold for the wider knowledge check. These thresholds are programme-design proposals, not validated measures of competence.

Record actual study time, confusing explanations, access failures, pre/post scenario results and a short retention check after two to four weeks. A completion badge alone should not be treated as permission to delegate actions to an agent. Choose the longer route only where better learning or a failed quality review justifies its additional time.

First draft for the executive committee

We recommend finalising Foundation as a concise blended programme. An internal showcase will cover our current AI use cases and the implications for colleagues’ work. The remaining curriculum will focus on understanding AI systems and agents, recognising their limitations, and using them responsibly under the bank’s rules.

The proposed external core combines selected lessons from Anthony Alicea’s AI Literacy for the Modern Workplace, already available through Udemy, with Microsoft’s introductory AI and agents module. A focused internal session will complete the agent-safety requirements and explain approved tools, information handling, permitted delegation and escalation. AI security threats will be covered in a separate awareness session using bank-relevant examples.

The preferred route requires approximately 4 hours 40 minutes per learner excluding the use-case showcase; using the complete Udemy course increases this to approximately 5 hours 25 minutes. No additional course licence purchase is expected for this route, subject to confirming the existing Udemy entitlement. Internal preparation and learner time remain real costs.

We propose a short pilot before wider assignment, with scenario-based checks of safe decisions and understanding. Anthropic’s deeper capabilities course is the preferred alternative if it delivers materially better learning or the Udemy content fails the quality review. We do not currently recommend purchasing a broad DeepLearning.AI programme for all staff solely to meet Foundation, because the additional content does not eliminate the bank-specific gaps.

The committee is asked to endorse this direction, the pilot and protected learner time, with the final lesson list and rollout confirmed after the pilot.

Evidence and catalogue follow-up

The retained evidence extract preserves the existing assessments for 16 relevant catalogue candidates and all 26 Foundation outcomes outside N1. It is not a rescore. No catalogue, planner selections or approved learning requirements have been changed.

Before incorporating this recommendation into the planner:

  • Refresh TR-145: the current Anthropic site establishes a 3½-hour duration and more explicit behaviour exercises than the old “short, not stated” record.
  • Refresh TR-089 to the current four-hour listing and review the now-accessible practice content; do not automatically carry historical gaps into a fresh assessment.
  • Preserve the distinction between DeepLearning.AI and Coursera durations and entitlements. Do not label either estimate “video time” unless the provider identifies it that way.
  • Treat the 37-minute Microsoft module and any selected-course playlist as separate delivery units requiring their own scoped assessment. Do not transfer full-path scores to a subset of its lessons.
  • Review current Udemy videos in the organisation’s tenant. Public availability and the retained internal link are not proof of current licence entitlement or identical lesson versions.
  • Assess the finished internal materials before awarding coverage. A proposed session title, duration or mapping does not itself earn credit.

The recommendation reflects content available on 31 October 2026. The most consequential unresolved items are the quality of the selected Udemy videos, current tenant access, the bank’s approved product configurations, and whether the proposed local sessions achieve the intended outcomes within the allocated time.
