L208: ## Description
L209: 
L210: Today, AI isn't just a futuristic idea. It's already integrated into many of the tools you use every day, whether you realize it or not. It helps filter spam in your email, powers customer support chatbots, and even suggests who to connect with on professional networks. Our company is embracing AI to remain competitive and innovative, but with that comes a shared responsibility.
L211: This isn't just an internal guideline; it's a global mandate. Regulations like the EU AI Act - Article 4, the U.S. Executive Orders, and India’s DPDP Act are shaping how businesses must use this technology. To stay compliant and protect our company's integrity, every one of us must understand what's safe, what's risky, and what's prohibited when using AI.
L212: Every employee is a user of AI, and misusing it—even by accident—can have serious consequences.
L213:   * Accidentally pasting sensitive client data into a public AI tool could lead to a major data breach.
L214: 
L215:   * Using AI without proper oversight can introduce bias into our work, harming our reputation and fairness.
L216:   * Failing to comply with regulations could expose our company to significant legal and financial risks.
L217: On the other hand, employees who understand how to use AI responsibly and smartly will gain a huge advantage. You can save hours on routine tasks, improve accuracy in your work, and unlock new ideas and innovations.This course is designed to equip you with the knowledge to be both productive and compliant, turning a potential risk into a powerful opportunity.
L218: This course is structured to build your knowledge from the ground up.
L219: 
L220:   * Modules 1–5 will build your foundational knowledge. We’ll explore what AI is, identify common risks, and review key global laws and our internal policies.
L221: 
L222:   * Modules 6–7 will focus on practical application. This is where we get hands-on with effective and responsible ways to collaborate with AI tools.
L223:   * Module 8 is our capstone. You'll get to apply everything you've learned through real-world scenarios and a final quiz to test your mastery.
