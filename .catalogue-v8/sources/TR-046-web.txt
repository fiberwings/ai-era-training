L54: “AI is the new electricity” proclaims Andrew Ng, co-founder of Google Brain. Just as we need to know how to safely harness electricity, we also need to know how to securely employ Artificial Intelligence (AI) to power our businesses. In some scenarios, the security of AI systems can impact the safety and well-being of humans. Hence, it is imperative that AI systems, including the models and algorithms, be protected against cyber-attacks.
L55: On the flip side, AI techniques can also be misused by cyber-adversaries and so we need to understand how to counter them.
L56: Cybersecurity professionals, in partnership with AI specialists, would need to enhance their organisations’ cybersecurity strategy and frameworks to take in account the following cybersecurity implications of AI:
L57: ● Security, safety and privacy of AI systems deployed in various business use cases.
L58: ● Strengths and shortcomings of employing AI for cybersecurity defence.
L59: ● AI-aided attack techniques and corresponding mitigations / compensating controls.

L65: ### Key Takeaways
L66: 
L67: At the end of the course, learners will be able to:
L68: 
L69: ● Identify security, safety and privacy vulnerabilities of AI systems, including AI-specific cybersecurity risks, and recommend appropriate risk mitigation actions and recognise limitations, if any
L70: 
L71: ● Evaluate the appropriateness of AI for cybersecurity defence and identify their shortcomings, if any.
L72: 
L73: ● Describe various AI-aided attack techniques and recommend appropriate mitigations or compensating controls.
L74: ● Keep abreast of new developments in AI/ML security in order to assess the risks, if any, they pose to their organisation.

L77: ### Structure
L78: 
L79: ● Course duration: 6 weeks online.
L80: ● Learners' time commitment: 3 to 4 hours per week.
L81: ● This course has 4 live online sessions for group activities, discussions, feedback and interactions.
L82: ● Throughout the course, learners are expected to actively contribute to the group activities and online discussions, and are encouraged to apply the knowledge learnt.

L85: ### Who Should Attend
L86: 
L87: This course is ideal for professionals in Singapore and around the world, as it is conducted entirely online.
L88: 
L89: This course is suitable for leaders and professionals in cyber security, cyber risk and AI who need to:
L90: ● Formulate security policies, strategies or programmes or any related action plan to address the risks posed by using AI-based systems in their business processes and/or products.
L91: ● Understand the implications of AI-powered attack techniques to their cyber defences.
L92: ● Assess the appropriateness of AI-aided cyber defence solutions and their shortcomings, if any.
