L208: ## Description
L209: 
L210: Artificial Intelligence, or AI, and Generative AI, are all the rage these days. Everyone's talking about revolutionary tools like ChatGPT, GPT-4, Perplexity, Gemini, CoPilot, etc.
L211: 
L212: The technology behind AI is complex and amazing. But what’s probably even more complex, and nuanced, is creating and using AI for Ethical Decisions, and using the right data to train the AI correctly. And thats what you will learn from this Course.
L213: I am well aware of the technical basics of AI, having spent nearly 2 decades Leading and Managing Multiple Global Projects in the Software Industry.
L214: 
L215: But my expertise, and passion, lie in the area of Decision Making, Ethics, and Leadership Psychology.
L216: 
L217: So, this Course isn't about the technical nitty-gritty of AI, although I will be explaining the basics of AI, how it works etc. in simple terms. My focus will be in my areas of expertise, Decision Making and Ethics.
L218: I will be looking at AI, from an Ethical Decision Making point of view. To help Leaders drive the creation, and use, of AI responsibly ! This Course is primarily for Leaders, but everyone can enjoy it, and learn something new from it !
L219: 
L220: There will be several examples and Case Studies, as well as a few Practice Exercises and Quizzes, to help you learn better from the Course.
L221: 
L222: AI Tools only used for minor language edits, and in searching for Research Papers and Sources.
L223: ## Who this course is for:
L224: 
L225:   * Leaders, involved in Driving Initiatives for the Creation, Implementation, and Use of AI Systems
L226:   * Anyone involved in Initiatives for the Creation, Implementation, and Use of AI Systems or Programs/Tools
L227:   * Anyone interested in gaining insights into the many ways AI can go wrong, and how we can avoid those pitfalls
