L161: Course Overview:
L162: "Responsible AI: A Non-Technical Guide for Managers" povides a high-level introduction and is tailored to equip managers with the knowledge and tools necessary to navigate the complexities of AI implementation responsibly. The course provides both key concepts, some initial solutions and various cautionary tales to make the issue more tangible.
L163:

L165: 
L166:   1. Intro to Machine Learning, AI and Responsible AI
L167:   2. Core Principles of Responsible AI related to privacy, fairness and transparency
L168: 
L169:   3. Generative AI Risks as hallucinations, data leakage and bias
L170: 
L171:   4. Advanced Topics in Responsible AI related to sustainability, AGI and ASI
L172: 
L173:   5. Implementing Responsible AI in Practice focusing on the responsible AI board, principles and training
L174: 
L175: Why Enroll?
L176: 
L177: This course offers a comprehensive, non-technical approach, making it accessible to managers from diverse backgrounds.
L178: Through real-world examples and practical insights, you'll gain the confidence to lead AI initiatives that are not only innovative but also ethically sound and sustainable.
L179: 
L180: Whether you're new to AI or looking to deepen your understanding of its ethical implications, this course equips you to lead responsibly in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
L181: ## Who this course is for:
L182:   * Business Leaders and Decision-Makers: Managers, executives, and entrepreneurs seeking to understand and guide responsible AI practices in their organizations without technical prerequisites.
L183:   * Professionals in Data-Driven Industries: Those working in sectors like finance, HR, healthcare, education, or government services, where AI governance and ethical considerations are critical.
L184:   * Compliance and Risk Officers: Individuals navigating AI-related regulatory frameworks, governance policies, and risk mitigation strategies.
L185:   * AI Project Stakeholders: Anyone involved in the oversight, implementation, or adoption of AI systems, focused on aligning technology with ethical and business goals.
