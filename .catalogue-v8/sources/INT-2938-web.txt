L141: ## Description
L142: 
L143: The European Union’s Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) is the world’s first major law designed to regulate AI. It sets out a comprehensive, risk-based framework that defines how AI can be developed, deployed, and used across Europe. More than just another piece of legislation, this Act will shape global standards for trustworthy and responsible AI—and influence businesses, developers, and governments worldwide.
L144: This course is designed to give you a clear, practical overview of the EU AI Act without overwhelming you with legal jargon. In under an hour, you’ll learn how the Act works, why it was created, and what concrete steps organizations need to take to prepare. You’ll gain insight into both the “big picture” goals of the law and the practical, day-to-day impact on compliance, innovation, and business strategy.
L145: 
L146: By the end of this course, you’ll be able to:
L147:   * Explain the scope, structure, and objectives of the EU AI Act
L148: 
L149:   * Understand the four AI risk categories (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal) and what they mean in practice
L150: 
L151:   * Recognize compliance obligations for providers, deployers, and users of AI systems

L154: 
L155:   * Assess how the Act affects different industries and AI use cases
L156:   * Anticipate how global markets may adopt similar frameworks, and what this means for the future of AI
L157: Whether you are a developer building AI systems, a business leader planning strategy, a compliance officer preparing for audits, or a policymaker following global trends, this course will give you the knowledge and confidence to navigate the EU AI Act effectively. You’ll walk away with a solid understanding of how the Act works, what it demands, and how to align your projects with its requirements, so you can stay ahead of the curve in this new era of AI regulation.
