L179: 
L180: IMPORTANT!
L181: 
L182: Before you embark on this course, it's essential to understand its scope and purpose to be sure it aligns with your expectations.
L183: 
L184: This course is designed with a specific audience in mind: decision-makers with leading and managerial positions, who want to understand AI, and what to do with so-called AI "trend", and explore it's potential in business.
L185: Your employees might be either enthusiastic or nervous about AI active development. Let's explore if there are reasons behind these feelings.
L186: 
L187: Here's what you should expect:
L188: 1. A General Overview: This course provides a broad introduction to the world of AI, focusing on Generative AI. It's NOT intended to delve deeply into technical aspects. It's NOT about tips for ChatGPT or Google Bard. BUT! Instead it offers a comprehensive understanding of the WHY behind using AI in a business context. It's about intersection of AI and leadership. It equips you with the knowledge needed to initiate conversations and projects related to AI within your team or organization.
L189: 2. Strategic Insight: You will gain valuable insights into the strategic implications of implementing AI in your organization. This knowledge will empower you to make informed decisions when considering AI integration projects.
L190: 
L191: 3. Tools and Chatbot Overviews: I do provide an overview of Generative AI tools (most popular and those enhancing productivity), and explanations of chatbots (NOT how to work with them). This can help you understand their potential applications.
L192: 4. No Prescriptive Solutions: Keep in mind that this course will not offer step-by-step solutions EXACTLY for YOUR specific business needs. Instead, the focus is on WHY, WHEN, and WHAT to take into account to consider AI.
L193: 
L194: 5. Business-Centric Approach: This course is tailored to business, emphasizing the business value of AI. It encourages you to think strategically and make informed decisions in alignment with your unique business goals.
L195: 
L196: As a result,

L201:   * If your top managers decide to introduce AI innovations into your organization, you'll be equipped to engage in meaningful discussions and assess the potential impact.
