L145: ## Description
L146: 
L147: Artificial Intelligence is transforming every industry — but true leadership in this new era requires more than technical know-how. It calls for wisdom, empathy, and a human-centered approach.

L149: In this course, you’ll explore how to lead responsibly in an AI-driven world. Drawing on psychology, ethics, and design thinking, you’ll learn how to align intelligent systems with human values — ensuring technology supports growth, wellbeing, and fairness rather than undermining them.
L150: You’ll discover frameworks for responsible AI decision-making, explore practical cases from healthcare, education, creativity, and sustainability, and learn how to measure success in human terms — trust, empowerment, and social impact. You will learn what the frontiers of human-centered AI, including social AI, creative AI and emotional AI.
