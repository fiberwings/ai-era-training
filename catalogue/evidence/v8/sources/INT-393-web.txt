L83: 
L84:   * AI Risks Preview 11:23

L88:   * The Basics of AI 10:22

L91:   * Types of AI Systems 15:36
L92: 
L93: Explore the varieties of AI systems, from AI agents that perceive inputs and act to symbolic and subsymbolic approaches, and learning paradigms from supervised to reinforcement learning.
L94: 
L95:   * Classification of AI According to the EU AI Act 10:21
L96: Explore the EU AI act’s risk-based classification from minimal to unacceptable risk, and learn how high-risk systems demand transparency, risk assessments, data quality, human oversight, and robust documentation.

L98:   * Ethical Principles of AI 7:46
L99:

L102: 
L103:   * ISO 42001 AI Management Systems 28:46
L104:

L108: Explore the context and scope of an ai management system under iso 42001, define objectives, assess risks, assign roles, secure resources, and pursue continuous improvement.

L110:   * Annexes of ISO 42001 AI Management Systems 2:43
L111: 
L112: Explore ISO 42,001 annexes for AI governance and management systems: annex A's applicability and controls with justification, annex B's implementation guidance, and annex C's AI risk sources for risk assessment.
L113: 
L114:   * Roles and Responsibilities in an AIMS 10:15
L115: Explore the full spectrum of roles in AI governance and management systems, from AI manager and ethics officer to data science, risk, user experience, and policy analysts.
L116: 
L117:   * Standards and Laws over AI 7:05
L118: 
L119: Examine AI governance standards and laws, including ISO 42,001; NIST AI risk management framework; and EU AI act, with focus on certification, risk, transparency, and terminology.

L142: ## Description
L143: 
L144: This course is designed to equip professionals with the skills and knowledge to manage AI systems within their organizations, mitigate risks, and ensure compliance with frameworks. Led by Adrian Resag, an experienced AI governance and risk management expert, this course explores AI's role in corporate governance, data analytics, risk assessment, and compliance strategies.
L145: Participants will gain hands-on insights into AI-powered risk management, ethical considerations, and AI maturity models. The course covers everything from basic AI concepts to the implementation of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS), providing practical guidance on integrating AI into business operations.
L146: 
L147: What You Will Learn
L148: 
L149:   * Introduction to Artificial Intelligence – Understanding AI fundamentals and classifications.
L150:   * AI in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) – Exploring AI’s role in information security, data management, and regulatory compliance.
L151: 
L152:   * AI Data Analytics – Using AI for risk detection, compliance monitoring, and advanced data analysis.
L153: 
L154:   * Planning & Implementing AI Management Systems – Step-by-step guidance on integrating AI governance frameworks, including ISO 42001.
L155: 
L156:   * AI Risk Management & Ethical Principles – Understanding AI risks, ethical AI frameworks, and maturity models.
L157:   * AI Auditing & Compliance – Applying ISO 42001, understanding regulatory requirements, and auditing AI systems.
L158: 
L159: This course includes practical exercises, real-world case studies, and step-by-step implementation strategies to ensure you can effectively apply AI governance principles in your organization. Whether you are a risk manager, auditor, compliance officer, or business leader, this course will help you leverage AI while ensuring responsible and compliant deployment.
