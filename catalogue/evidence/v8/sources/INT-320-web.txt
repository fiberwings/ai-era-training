L216: Welcome to the program on AI for CxO's and Sr. Managers.
L217: Everywhere you look, from global headlines to bustling startups, the impact of AI and Machine Learning is undeniable. Especially after the buzz surrounding revolutionary advancements like ChatGPT, there's no longer any doubt: we're in the age of AI.
L218: Ever felt overwhelmed by the storm of terminologies like Large Language Models (LLM), Machine Learning, Generative AI, Transformers, or Strong vs. Weak AI? Or felt that gnawing concern that your competitors might be leveraging AI in ways you haven’t tapped into yet? This course is the remedy to your FOMO (fear of missing out).

L220: Dive in with us to:
L221: 
L222:   1. Understand the three waves of AI - Predictive, Generative, and Agentic AI - and why leaders must understand their differences, capabilities, and risks.
L223:   2. Distinguish between AI Agents and Agentic AI, and explore how AI is moving from answering questions to autonomously executing tasks and workflows.
L224: 
L225:   3. Get a tech-light refresher of core AI concepts relevant for leaders, including machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, transformers, RAG, and explainable AI.
L226: 
L227:   4. Understand the growing challenges of a multi-LLM enterprise world, where teams use different AI models with varying behaviors, costs, risks, and outputs.
L228:   5. Explore how machine learning inside Excel, powered by Claude, is changing business analysis, forecasting, anomaly detection, and executive reporting.
L229: 
L230:   6. Learn how AI is accelerating decision-making at scale, and why governance, accountability, and AI risk management are becoming boardroom priorities.
L231: 
L232:   7. Discuss the hidden risks of AI including bias, hallucinations, privacy concerns, invisible decision risks, and governance gaps as AI adoption explodes across organizations.
L233:   8. Explore the rise of vibe coding, where business users can rapidly build applications, automations, and workflows using natural language instead of traditional coding approaches.

L235:   9. Discover how leaders can use AI for fraud detection, demand planning, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, customer experience, and operational intelligence through practical business case studies.
