L226: Artificial intelligence is increasingly influencing decisions in business, education, healthcare, finance, hiring, and everyday life. While AI can process information quickly and generate confident recommendations, it cannot fully understand human values, context, ethics, or responsibility.
L227: This course provides a practical framework for using human judgment effectively in an AI-driven world. You will learn how to evaluate AI-generated information, recognize bias, question assumptions, check evidence, and make responsible decisions without becoming overly dependent on algorithms.
L228: You’ll explore how human intuition, reasoning, experience, and values work alongside AI. Through practical exercises and realistic scenarios, you will learn when to trust AI-supported insights, when to investigate further, and when human expertise should take priority.
