L328: ## Description
L329: 
L330: Updated in 2026
L331: 
L332: The course has been re-recorded and reorganized into a clear six-part path, based on feedback from thousands of students. New content includes Microsoft Presidio, Penn Testing AI Agent and OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications.
L333: 
L334: Your GenAI app will be attacked. Will your guardrails hold?
L335: In IBM's 2025 Cost of a Data Breach report, 13% of organizations reported a breach involving an AI model or application, and 97% of those had no proper AI access controls. Chatbots have been talked into selling a car for $1, a parcel-delivery bot was coaxed into swearing at its customers, and an airline was held liable for what its chatbot invented. This course teaches you to prevent those failures, step by step and hands-on.
L336: 
L337: How the course is organized
L338: The course follows the path of a request through a GenAI application, tests and hardens its guardrails, then shows how to build and secure AI agents. Every part builds on the one before:
L339: 
L340:   1. Foundations. How LLMs, inference parameters, embeddings, RAG and prompts work, and the risks they create, mapped to the OWASP Top 10.
L341: 
L342:   2. Defend the pipeline
L343: 
L344:      * Input: stop prompt injection with Prompt Guard and Llama Guard, including attacks hidden in images (Llama Guard 3 Vision). OWASP LLM01.
L345:      * Output: detect hallucinations with judge models and Vectara's evaluation model. OWASP LLM09.
L346: 
L347:      * Data: find and anonymize PII with Microsoft Presidio. OWASP LLM02.
L348: 
L349:   3. Test. Prove your guardrails work with faithfulness, SAS and context-relevance evaluators and a full RAG evaluation in Haystack, then red-team the model with Garak (encoding, data exfiltration and profanity probes).
L350:   4. Build. Combine defenses with GuardrailsAI (validators, RAIL, on-fail policies) and NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails (Colang, runtime), then do the same with managed Amazon Bedrock Guardrails and Bedrock evaluations.
L351: 
L352:   5. AI agents. Build an AI pentest agent with CrewAI and OWASP ZAP, then a bonus multimodal ReAct agent, and learn how agents connect to tools through MCP.
L353:   6. Secure agentic AI. Work through all ten risks in the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (2026), from Agent Goal Hijack to Rogue Agents. Each risk comes with real-world scenarios, a technology map, a defense architecture and a knowledge check, and the course ends with a capstone assessment.
