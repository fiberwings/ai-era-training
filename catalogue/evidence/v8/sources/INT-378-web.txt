L203: Unlock the power of AI to detect anomalies, fraud, and suspicious behaviour in digital systems. "AI for Suspicious Activity Monitoring" is a hands-on, end-to-end course designed to teach you how to use traditional AI techniques, deep learning, and generative AI (GenAI) to monitor and respond to unusual patterns in real-world data.
L204: Whether you're a developer, data analyst, or aspiring AI professional, this course provides practical tools and strategies to build intelligent monitoring systems using Python, autoencoders, and large language models (LLMs).
L205: 
L206: What You’ll Learn
L207:

L210: 
L211:   * GenAI & LLM Integration: Explore how large language models like OpenAI’s GPT and frameworks such as LangChain and LLAMA-Index can assist in monitoring human-generated activity (e.g., suspicious conversations, document scans).
L212:   * Fraud and Cyber Threat Detection: Apply AI tools to detect threats in finance, cybersecurity, e-commerce, and other high-risk domains.
L213: 
L214:   * Cloud-Based Implementation: Build scalable pipelines using tools like Google Colab for real-time or batch monitoring.
L215: 
L216:   * Text Analysis for Audit Trails: Perform NLP-based extraction, entity recognition, and text summarisation to flag risky interactions and records.
