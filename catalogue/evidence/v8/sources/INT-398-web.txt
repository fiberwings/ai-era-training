L198: ## Description
L199: 
L200: This course contains the use of artificial intelligence.
L201: 
L202: Today’s companies are adopting AI faster than they are learning to manage it.
L203: 
L204: Marketing teams generate content with AI. Sales teams draft proposals. Customer support uses AI to respond to clients. Product teams rely on AI for documentation and analysis. This adoption happens naturally — often without formal approval, visibility, or defined responsibility.
L205: As a result, AI becomes part of the company’s operating system before the management system is ready.
L206: 
L207: For a CEO or executive, this creates a new leadership challenge. The issue is no longer whether AI is used. The issue is whether that usage is controlled, visible, and aligned with business objectives.
L208: 
L209: This course teaches exactly that: how to turn distributed, informal AI usage into a structured governance system.
L210: You will learn how to see where AI is already integrated into your processes, how to classify data and risks, how to define governance rules, assign ownership, implement approval workflows, manage incidents, and create executive-level reporting.
L211: 
L212: This is not a course about AI tools.
L213: This is a course about management architecture.
L214: By the end of the program, AI will no longer be an informal employee productivity tool. It will become a managed, accountable, and scalable part of your company’s operating system.
