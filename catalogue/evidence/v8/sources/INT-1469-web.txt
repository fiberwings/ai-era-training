L172: ## Description
L173: 
L174: Updated August 2026. Includes the Digital Omnibus changes (Regulation 2026/1744) that rewrote the AI Act's deadlines in July 2026.
L175: 
L176: The EU AI Act is being enforced right now. Transparency rules, the penalty regime and the enforcement authorities all switched on in August 2026, with fines reaching 35 million euros or 7% of worldwide turnover. Most companies still do not know which of their systems are affected.
L177: This course takes you from the legal text to a working compliance program, with the templates to run it.
L178: 
L179: What you will be able to do
L180: 
L181:   * Decide whether the Act applies to you, including if your company sits outside the EU
L182: 
L183:   * Identify your role for each system, and spot when your role changes without you noticing
L184: 
L185:   * Screen any system against the ten prohibited practices, including the two added for generative AI in 2026
L186:   * Classify high-risk systems through both Annex I and Annex III, and apply the Article 6(3) filter to legitimately exit
L187: 
L188:   * Meet the Article 50 transparency rules that are already enforceable today
L189: 
L190:   * Build an inventory, a governance structure and a 90-day plan sized to your organization
L191: What you download
L192: Every section ships with working files, not slides: a quick reference card with all the dates and fines, an applicability and role-finder kit, a classification engine in Excel, a self-assessment template for Article 6(4), a requirements tracker with separate provider and deployer tabs, a FRIA template with DPIA cross-references, ready-to-use disclosure texts, a vendor questionnaire, and the full inventory workbook with the 90-day tracker.
L193: What makes this course different
L194: Most AI Act training covers providers and stops there. For every company that builds a high-risk system, hundreds simply use one. Article 26 gets a full lesson here, and the deployer obligations run through every toolkit. The course also teaches the two prohibitions added by the Omnibus, applicable from December 2026, which most training programs have not caught up with yet.
L195: Who this is for
L196: Compliance, legal, risk, product and technology professionals. No legal background required.
