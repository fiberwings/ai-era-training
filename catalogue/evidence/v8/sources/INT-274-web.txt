L328: You’ll participate in collaborative exercises—rethinking how AI design choices can foster, or hinder, justice and inclusion worldwide.
L329: Risk Management, Ethics Frameworks, and Human Oversight
L330: Risks in generative AI deployment are complex and evolving. You’ll get hands-on with industry-standard risk assessment frameworks, learning to identify risks proactively during the AI development lifecycle. Our ethical decision-making modules go beyond checklists, immersing you in role-playing exercises, stakeholder negotiation, and portfolio-ready scenario analysis—a unique opportunity to “try on” the role of ethicist, developer, and regulator in live simulation.

We’ll compare models of governance, delineate the “human-in-the-loop” paradigm, and equip you to intervene effectively when automated decisions go sideways, ensuring every future AI system you touch is human-centered by design.
