L218: The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (RMF) Masterclass is an essential course for professionals navigating the complex landscape of AI risk management. L219: This course offers an in-depth exploration of the National Institute of Standards and Technology's AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF), providing practical insights for its application in corporate environments.
