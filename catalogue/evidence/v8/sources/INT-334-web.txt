L290: ## Description
L291: 
L292: AI is changing how helping professionals work. This course makes sure you're ready.
L293: 
L294: Whether you're a therapist, coach, healthcare professional, social worker, teacher, or HR professional, this course gives you practical, ethical guidance grounded in the realities of your work.
L295: Written and taught by a chartered psychologist and software engineer, it gives you everything you need to use AI confidently, safely, and ethically in your professional practice. We start from the very basics, so you don't need any prior knowledge of AI to get started.
L296: 
L297: You will learn:
L298: 
L299:   * How to use AI for research, formulation, report writing, note-taking, and summarising documents
L300: 
L301:   * How to craft effective prompts to get useful, reliable output
L302:   * How to navigate ethical issues such as competence, privacy, hallucinations, bias and deskilling
L303: 
L304:   * How to run AI locally so client data never leaves your computer
L305: 
L306: We also cover territory that most AI courses ignore entirely: what to do when your clients are using AI. Whether a client arrives with a ChatGPT formulation, uses AI for reassurance between sessions, or has developed an unhealthy relationship with an AI companion, you will know how to respond.
L307: By the end of the course, you will be able to use AI as a skilled professional assistant while keeping your clinical judgement, your ethics, and your clients' wellbeing at the centre of your practice.
L308: ## Who this course is for:
L309:   * Counsellors, psychotherapists, psychologists, coaches, social workers, and teachers
L310:   * Mindfulness teachers, occupational therapists, and other allied health professionals
L311:   * Any helping professional who handles sensitive client data and wants to use AI responsibly
L312:   * HR professionals, managers, and supervisors supporting staff who work with vulnerable people
L313:   * Complete beginners with no prior knowledge of AI — we start from scratch
L314:   * Those already using AI who want to ensure they are doing so safely and ethically
