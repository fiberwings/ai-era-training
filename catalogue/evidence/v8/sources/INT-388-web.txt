L128: ## Description
L129: 
L130: Artificial Intelligence is no longer just for technologists. It is rapidly becoming a core workplace skill. From writing emails and reports to preparing presentations and analyzing information, AI tools are changing how professionals work every day. Whether you use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Co-Pilot, or another tool, this course has practical tips for you.
L131: AI Fundamentals for the Workplace is a short, practical course (about 30 mins) designed to help business professionals understand what AI is and how to begin using it confidently at work. In about 30 minutes, you will learn the essential concepts behind artificial intelligence and see real examples of how AI tools can help you save time and improve productivity.
L132: This course avoids technical jargon and focuses on what professionals actually need to know. You will learn how AI works at a high level, where it is already being used across industries, and how it can support common workplace tasks such as writing, summarizing information, brainstorming ideas, and organizing projects.
L133: You will also practice one of the most important new skills in the modern workplace: prompting. Prompting is the ability to ask AI the right questions so you receive clearer, faster, and more useful responses. Along the way, you will learn how to avoid common mistakes, use AI responsibly, and think about AI as a partner that can help free up your time for the work that requires human judgment, creativity, and relationships.
L134: By the end of this course, you will not only understand the fundamentals of artificial intelligence, you will have experimented with it yourself on real workplace tasks. The goal is simple: to give you the confidence and foundation you need to start using AI tools effectively as they continue to evolve.
