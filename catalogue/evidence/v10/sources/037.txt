What you'll learn
Statistics for real-world decision-making:
Learn to calculate and interpret descriptive and inferential statistics to solve business problems.

: Design clear, compelling visualizations that effectively communicate complex data stories to stakeholders.
Generative AI for analytics
: Leverage generative AI in the data analytics lifecycle, with hands-on labs and guidance on when and how to use AI assistance.
Why Enroll?
Data-informed decision-making has become an essential skill across nearly every role and industry. As reliance on data grows, so does demand for professionals who can analyze and interpret it effectively, with data science roles projected to grow 34% from 2024 to 2034 according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
Led by industry leader Sean Barnes, the Data Analytics Professional Certificate equips you with the skills to manage the entire data lifecycle, from defining problems to delivering insights. You'll start with the foundations of analysis, visualization, and statistical reasoning, then build Python skills to automate workflows and work with data at scale (no prior coding experience required). From there, you'll extract data from websites, APIs, and databases, clean it with Python and SQL, and learn to present your findings through clear writing, compelling visualizations, and interactive dashboards.
What sets this program apart is its integration of AI into the analytics workflow. You'll learn to use large language models as a thought partner for simulation modeling, formula debugging, and visualization — building practical skills through real-world examples. Whether you're entering the field or expanding an existing toolkit, you'll graduate prepared to pursue data analytics roles or strengthen your current one with a solid analytical foundation.
You’ll earn a certificate for individual courses, and certifying in all of them as they become available will grant you the Data Analytics Professional Certificate

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the course?
The course is designed to be completed in about a month, with an estimated commitment of 5 hours per week.
Can I take this course at my own pace?
Yes, the course is designed to be self-paced.
What kind of support is available?
Access to discussion forums, detailed documentation, and resources to support your learning.
Is there a certificate upon completion?
You will receive a certificate at the end of each course if you pay or receive financial aid for the courses and complete the assessments. There is a limit of 180 days of certificate eligibility, after which you must re-purchase the course to obtain a certificate. If you audit the course for free, you will not receive a certificate.
If you complete all 3 courses in the Professional Certificate, you will also receive an additional certificate showing that you completed the entire Professional Certificate.
I have questions about my subscription of this Professional Certificate, whom can I ask?
Please use the
Coursera Learner Help Center
for questions about your subscription.
How much does this Professional Certificate cost?
A Coursera subscription costs $49 / month.
Can I apply for financial aid?
Yes, Coursera provides financial aid to learners who cannot afford the cost of a subscription.
Can I preview the Professional Certificate?
> Yes! You can preview the course for free by accessing the entire first module at no cost. This allows you to explore the learning experience before deciding if you’d like to continue. If you want full access to all modules, assessments, and the certificate of completion, you’ll need to upgrade to the paid version.
