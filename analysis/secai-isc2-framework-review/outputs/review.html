Catalogue implications

ISC2's bundle is already TR-014; TR-015 is its AI for Cybersecurity component and must not receive duplicate credit. Its current recorded whole-framework coverage is 3.7%; this deeper public-page review supports 13.6%. TR-023 is the SecAI+ exam, while TR-155 is a separate Pluralsight preparation path. Neither is interchangeable with the official CertMaster Perform offer assessed here. The new evidence warrants revisiting TR-014 and assessing official training as a distinct record. Existing records, assessments and app snapshots have not been changed.

Recommended use: choose SecAI+ for experienced cyber staff needing broad AI-security grounding; choose ISC2 for a lighter strategic introduction, particularly human oversight and workforce change. Add targeted bank-context training and hands-on agent use, supervision, Skills and evaluation. These additions need their own assessed evidence before they earn coverage.

Method and audit

0 = no specific public teaching evidence; 1 = mention/touch; 2 = taught but incomplete or lacking required practice; 3 = the complete outcome supported. Knowledge outcomes can reach 3 without labs; practical outcomes require suitable exercise evidence. No practical outcome was given 3 merely because the course has labs or case studies. Whole-framework coverage is the equal-weight mean of 23 need percentages. Within each need: sum of scores ÷ (3 × outcome count). Combined coverage uses each outcome's best score, never addition.

Must-have fractions use outcome counts, with the denominator shown. All conditional must-haves are included for this full-framework comparison: N11.3: When automated judges are used; N14.7: For leadership responsibilities; R3.1.3: For multi-agent testing responsibilities; R6.1.2: When assurance work itself uses AI; R9.1.2: For delegated-identity integration responsibilities. Individual role pathways should apply their actual conditions. Coverage of all specialisms is a breadth comparison, not a demand that every employee study all of them.
