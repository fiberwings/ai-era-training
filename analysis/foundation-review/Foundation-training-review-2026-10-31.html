Compare the options

Scoring: 0 = no evidenced coverage; 1 = mention (⅓ credit); 2 = partial teaching (⅔ credit); 3 = full outcome (full credit). Skill outcomes require practice for 3. These use the catalogue’s existing 0–3 rubric.

  • Each need: sum of its outcome scores ÷ (3 × number of outcomes) × 100. Outcomes have equal weight within a need.
  • Each option: take the highest course score for each outcome, then calculate coverage. Overlap is counted once; two partial courses are not automatically treated as full coverage.
  • Priority N2–N4: average of N2, N3 and N4. Overall N2–N6: average of all five needs, including N5 and N6. Needs have equal weight. N1 is excluded because it is reserved for the in-house showcase; proposed internal content earns no credit.

Percentages are a weighted curriculum-coverage index, not measured competence, quality or time saved. Scores use retained catalogue evidence with documented public-content updates. Unlisted course outcomes score 0; that means coverage is not established by the reviewed evidence. Small differences should not decide the choice alone.
